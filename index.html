Account Management · Password & Access

How to Reset Your Password and Regain Account Access

Overview

If you're unable to log in, this guide will walk you through every step to regain access quickly. Most login issues are resolved in under 3 minutes.

Step-by-Step: Reset Your Password

  1. 1
    Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password" beneath the login button.
  2. 2
    Enter the email address you used when you signed up. Check your spam folder if you don't see the email within 2 minutes.
  3. 3
    Click the reset link in the email. Note: this link expires after 30 minutes.
  4. 4
    Create a new password. It must be at least 8 characters and include one number or symbol.
  5. 5
    Log in using your email and new password.

Still Can't Get In?

If resetting your password doesn't work, try these steps before contacting support:

  1. 1
    Open an incognito or private browsing window and try logging in there.
  2. 2
    Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
  3. 3
    Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again.
💡 Pro tip: If the incognito window works but your regular browser doesn't, the issue is almost always a browser extension (like an ad blocker or VPN). Try disabling extensions one at a time to find the culprit.

When to Contact Support

Contact us if: you're not receiving the reset email after 5 minutes (and it's not in spam), or you get an "account suspended" or "authentication error" message. Live chat is available Mon–Fri 9am–6pm EST.
